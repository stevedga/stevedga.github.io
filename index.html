MESSKAI

AI automation for repetitive internal work.

MESSKAI builds software that takes over the repetitive operational tasks a small business ends up doing by hand every week. The work that never gets assigned to anyone and quietly grows as the business does.

What that looks like

  • Pulling information together from several systems into one regular report
  • Sorting and routing incoming email so the right things get seen first
  • Research and data collection that currently runs on someone's afternoon
  • Follow-up and scheduling that depends on a person remembering

Each build is scoped to one specific task for one specific business. There is no product to sign up for and nothing to install on this site.

Internal tools

MESSKAI runs its own internal tooling, including an application that sends email from a MESSKAI Google account using the Gmail API. That application is used only by MESSKAI and is not offered to anyone else. How it handles data is described in the privacy policy.
